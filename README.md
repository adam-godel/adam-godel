### Hey, I'm Adam.

I am a senior undergraduate studying mathematics and computer science at Boston University. The primary goal of my research is to make computers run interesting and complex workloads under heavily constrained computational environments. I currently work on systems for quantum computation as well as secure computation.

I am a researcher in the BU CASP Systems Lab focusing on secure multiparty computation as well as a WISER Quantum Solutions Launchpad (QSL) team lead affiliated with the US Naval Nuclear Lab focusing on benchmarking quantum simulation algorithms. I am also the founder of BU Quantum, which hosts weekly seminars and workshops, and I co-lead BU Hack4Impact, a web development club.
